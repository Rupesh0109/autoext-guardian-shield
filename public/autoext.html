The problem with single sensors

Intelligence at the edge.

A single smoke or temperature sensor is either too slow or too easily fooled. AutoExt fuses thermal, gas, optical, electrochemical and vibration signals on an edge controller, so a real ignition event is separated from engine heat, exhaust and road noise in milliseconds — then suppressed automatically, at the source.

01

The Environment

Engine bays and battery enclosures are hostile: high ambient heat, vibration, fluids and EMI. AutoExt starts by continuously mapping the thermal and chemical baseline of the compartment.

02

Multi-Modal Sensing

Thermal (T), gas (G), optical flame (O), electrochemical (E) and vibration (V) nodes are distributed across the risk zones, each streaming independent evidence at high rate.

03

Sensor Fusion

An edge controller fuses every stream in real time. Cross-correlated signatures confirm ignition and reject false positives from normal heat, exhaust or road vibration.

04

Automated Response

On confirmation, the controller isolates the hazard zone, alerts the occupants and triggers targeted suppression at the source — before thermal runaway or cabin intrusion.

Applications

Two platforms. One safety layer.

ICE & Engine Bays

Fuel lines, turbochargers and wiring looms create layered ignition risk. AutoExt monitors the bay continuously and suppresses locally before flames reach the firewall.

EV & Battery Systems

Cell venting precedes thermal runaway by seconds. Gas and thermal fusion catches that precursor window and initiates pack-level isolation and cooling.

About Us

Engineers building the fire safety layer vehicles never had.

AutoExt is an early-stage hardware startup developing embedded detection and suppression systems for road vehicles — from prototype benches to production-intent modules.

The Core Problems We Solve

  • Late detection: conventional alarms trigger only once fire is already established.
  • False alarms: single-sensor systems confuse engine heat and exhaust with ignition.
  • No automated response: most vehicles rely entirely on a manual extinguisher.
  • EV blind spots: thermal runaway precursors go unmonitored inside battery packs.
